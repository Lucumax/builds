## Start here: the GPU cost model

An owner breaks even at **$2.34 per billable H100 hour and $3.49 per B200 hour**, against median list prices of $4.25 and $8.25. If prices fall 15 percent a year, the H100 stops covering its cost below about **76 percent utilization** and the B200 below about 58 percent. At NVIDIA's published best case throughput, a million output tokens cost about **$0.31 to serve on an H100 and $0.15 on a B200**.

Public sources, every assumption labelled, illustrative. The workbook has live formulas, 138 of 138 checks pass, and a second script recomputes the numbers. It also covers H200, GB200 NVL72, MI300X and a hypothetical GB300.
